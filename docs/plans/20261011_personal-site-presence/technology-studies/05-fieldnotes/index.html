Where does the problem really begin?

I want to understand what happens before a task reaches the software — and what happens after it leaves. Who needs the information? Where does the work slow down? What has someone learned to do by hand?

The useful question is rarely just “can we automate this?” It is “what should become easier for the person doing the work?” That is the thread I would follow from a process into its code.

One thought in the margin

A workaround is information, not just an inconvenience. I would want to ask what it protects before trying to remove it.
