The process perspective

Not a service menu.
A place to start thinking.

The same task.
Again. And again.

When a manual workflow keeps repeating, the first question isn’t “what can we automate?” It’s “what is this work really trying to do?” I look at the handoffs, exceptions and decisions before choosing a tool.

Where does the work stop moving — and who has to pick it up?

Built by doing.
