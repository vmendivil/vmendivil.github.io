## Original premise
A personal collection of authored visual editions around Victor's software practice and curiosities. Visitor browses substantial visual essays, then understands the person and how to work with him. This is **not** a branding agency, evidence of clients, or a services-sales clone. Its governing action is an editorial scroll into an edition—not room travel or command-index selection.

## Visual world
Execution palette reconciled after independent review: page-scale carbon/ink, soft ivory sans type, electric-yellow actions and selected editorial fields, with expansive variable-color original artwork spreads. The earlier aubergine/acid-coral palette was a research-agent proposal, not a user-pinned requirement; retaining the built black/yellow adaptation follows the actual selected Alphamark reference. This is a parent prototype-direction decision, not a claim of user approval of the finished prototype. Large intentionally composed objects/images can evoke problem-solving and technical craft. Do not adopt reference client marks, Monobotics dot-eye, mockups, gold folds or layouts literally. Rich imagery is the work, not abstract ornament behind generic card grids.

Large restrained sans headlines, tight but readable measures, clear metadata, low border density. Paired asymmetric images and open captions make the gallery; reading is full-width editorial sequence with deliberate alternation between wide art and narrow text. Not a repeated title/body/button tile family, fake terminal, or shared sibling CSS.

## Complete content grammar
- Opening: Victor Mendivil / software developer and problem solver. One concise personally written statement; no required shared slogan. Substantial original image occupies visual authority. Direct navigation Editions, Approach, About, Contact.
- Two featured editions lead to complete local reading routes: `From requirement to workable system` and `After-hours curiosity`. They are editorial descriptions of practice/interests, not real project case studies. Titles must not imply delivered client work.
- Practice: Dynamics 365 F&O / ERP and requirements-to-support lifecycle in generous essay sections with margin labels. Use prose about responsibility and collaboration; don't simulate numerical outcomes.
- Curiosity edition: Raspberry Pi/homelab and AI-assisted workflows/web-app interests, original clearly illustrative artwork and factual interest captions. Optional prototype illustrations remain labeled explorations.
- About: conversational plain essay, optional one inspected generated portrait secondary to content, no fabricated awards, certificates, testimonials, stars or client logo band.
- Working together: contained freelance scope and full-time employment; career interests are clearly distinct from contract offer. Explain remote/evening/asynchronous constraints without invented scheduling promises.
- Contact: large standalone typographic invitation and LinkedIn link https://www.linkedin.com/in/vmendivilaxdeveloper; not agency questionnaire, fake email, fake form or Clutch rating.
- Footer: concise original navigation and factual identity; no invented location coordinates, company deck or downloadable portfolio proof.

## Reading and navigation
An edition is a proper reading destination with heading, transparent illustrative status, specific factual prose, alternating original image plates and visible return to Editions. Long captions remain readable. Work examples can be thought experiments; do not manufacture clients, implementation details or outcomes. Native scroll and links first; gentle image reveals can be optional. No reel is needed unless an actual original meaningful sequence exists; a Play control cannot be an ornamental dead button.

Mobile: gallery becomes a strongly paced image/text column with actual text at 16px minimum, art aspect ratios deliberately recomposed, and a compact accessible menu. Do not preserve intrusive cookie/sticky conversion overlays from the reference. Contact always reachable without scrolling a 10,000px corporate home. Reduced motion leaves all editorial content visible. Menu has focus/close/Escape/return behavior, not just a full-screen black cover.

## Distinctness and acceptance
Avoid 21's monospace white identity sheet/index and 19's floors/projected portal. Avoid accepted LivingIndex's translucent organic window language and SmallWorlds collectible ceramics: this is authored large-format editorial artwork, not another art-object modal gallery. Every reading destination has its own composition; no shared sidebar or closing form across prototypes. Test required six viewports, actual keyboard/touch browsing, full edition reading, contact and return. Maintained image/render library allowed; no template CSS/agency assets. No build authorized by this research packet.
