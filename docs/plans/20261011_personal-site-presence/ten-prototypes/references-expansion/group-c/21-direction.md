## Original premise
Victor's openly browsable register of practice notes and interests. Visitor chooses a precise topic from a terse inventory, sees its image or text preview, and opens the full note. Distinct from 20's linear editorial journey and 19's spatial floor travel. No fake project chronology: there is no provided verified public project history.

## Visual world
Small bone-white identity colophon anchored at upper left on a deep cool ink background, an original sparse mono label voice, quiet graphite rules, and very large variable-color original preview images. White colophon and broad negative space create the contrast; images provide color, not a compulsory shared accent palette. Not a desktop operating system, CRT, fake console, HUD or surveillance UI.

Use genuinely readable body typography: mono can carry short labels while longer essays use a carefully selected companion text face if needed. Do not copy Fragment Mono's exact sizing just to reproduce the reference's small-text defect. Keep unusual scale contrast and strict alignment. Inventory selection is a thin active line and label, not rounded chips/cards. Original previews may be flat artwork, photography only if genuinely available/licensed, or authored renderings; never client website screenshots or the source artist's portrait/collage.

## Register contents and factual truth
Use topic/type or theme labels instead of fabricated years, project counts, awards or external commissions:
- Practice / ERP systems: Dynamics 365 F&O grounding and problem-solving context.
- Practice / The delivery chain: requirements, technical design, implementation, tests, deployment, support, coordination. Not simulated proof of a specific shipped engagement.
- Curiosity / Raspberry Pi and homelab: current interest, not invented measured uptime or architecture claims.
- Curiosity / AI-assisted work: interest in process and web-app experiments. Hypothetical visuals explicitly labeled illustrative explorations.
- Person / About Victor: warm first-person voice, one optional existing generated artwork after inspection, no self-brand myth or awards.
- Availability / Working together: employed full time; contained remote/asynchronous/evening freelance scope; technical-lead/senior career interests.

Header colophon gives Victor Mendivil and truthful role, direct About/Approach/Contact navigation and return to register. Do not invent ongoing project count or location clock. Theme/item index remains separate from navigation.

## Full reading surface
Selecting a register row updates the live preview and a clear visible active row. A second explicit Open note action or normal link leads to that full essay; no hover-only essential content. Reading page preserves colophon but gives prose enough width and visual hierarchy. Start with full-scale original plate/title, then short topic introduction, detailed text with section labels, related register notes, Back to register restoring selection/scroll. Metadata reflects factual topic/context, never fictional client, timeline or outcome.

About and availability are authored routes, not generic modal cards. Contact is a stark correspondence sheet with honest availability paragraph and genuine LinkedIn link https://www.linkedin.com/in/vmendivilaxdeveloper. Replace the reference's service/budget/recaptcha submission form; no email or delivery implementation supplied. No form transmission, account login, trackers or proprietary logos.

## Input, responsive and accessibility
Desktop split composition changes to a single-column register with image/title/type captions on mobile; index becomes an optional accessible contents list, not a shrunken permanently fixed side pane. Compact white header must not obscure first title or final paragraph. Bottom social links should flow in document or reserve space, unlike the reference overlap. Controls 44px, essays >=16px, visible keyboard focus, semantic links, optional keyboard preview shortcuts never sole navigation.

Reduced motion renders instant preview replacement; no meaningless ESC-effects ritual. Browser Back restores selected topic. Scroll restoration and direct route initialization must be deterministic. No horizontal wheel trap: current reference offers no evidence for such a requirement. Verify six supplied viewport classes, readable long note, About, contact, back/return, keyboard/touch where practical; physical Safari remains separately unverified.

## Distinctness and acceptance
Not accepted AfterHours' computer/workshop, FutureArchive's cobalt sculptural archive, or LivingIndex's organic art-window curation. Open Register is an economical identity colophon plus relational topic inventory and large previews; its visitor task is locating a note, not operating an OS or art-object modal. Keep its type scale, palette fields, imagery and closing contact distinct from 20. Shared maintained rendering libraries allowed, never template CSS/scaffold. Research only; no construction or public assembly authorized here.
