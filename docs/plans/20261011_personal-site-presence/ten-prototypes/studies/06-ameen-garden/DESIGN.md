# Design System: Quiet Current
## Overview
**Creative North Star: "Quiet Current"**
An original WebGL creekside grove carries the opening; the interface recedes into ordinary links. Reading continues as an unboxed peach essay, followed by pink interests and a pale-sage closing. Deep teal remains the ink rather than a repeated panel fill. Folded petal and leaf surfaces, tapered curved bark, gravel and moss terrain are physically lit with directional shadow maps. This is a stylized original landscape, not a photorealistic reproduction of Ameen.
**Key Characteristics:**
- Reflective water and layered foliage.
- Receding navigation, readable personal copy.
- Varied long-form sections rather than cards.
## Colors
River ink anchors text throughout the botanical essay. Peach, blossom and pale sage each own a full reading passage. The opening blends luminous peach sky into green water. Focus is a contrasting rose outline.
## Typography
Bellefair is a fine, open display voice; Karla supports conversational reading. Display is capped at 82px; mobile opening is clamp(46px,14vw,60px). Section headings are 48–72px, mobile 41–47px. Practice lead-ins are 31px Bellefair inline with 20px body, 28px with 18px body on phones. Substantial copy and the interest disclosure are at least 16px.
## Layout
Opening is 100svh with a 720px desktop minimum and 740px portrait minimum. At max-height 500px and min-width 701px, the opening becomes a 390px-minimum wide composition: 46px welcome at left, retained scene at right, compact top header and bottom controls. Practice shares its peach ground and follows a winding prose measure with inline serif lead-ins and a fine authored stem, not article rows. Interests and contact use sequential indented text rather than twin columns. At 700px, prose indents narrow and the note flows after the introduction. 7% safe-area-aware reading gutters; all native links/buttons have at least 44px hit heights. No fixed scene overlays the reading path, no body scroll lock, no modal.
## Elevation & Depth
Landscape depth uses world-space perspective, curved branching tube geometry, instanced folded leaf and petal meshes, eroded terrain and textured stones. A 2048px directional shadow map models sunlight; a mirrored-camera half-float render target feeds a distortion/Fresnel water shader with depth tint and surface-normal specular light. Interface panels have no elevation shadows. The practice margin uses an original fine SVG stem; no ripple outlines or card separators remain. Stylized geometry and the restrained sky are not claimed as reference-level cinematic optical fidelity.
## Shapes
No repeated card containers. Botanical forms are authored curved 3D surfaces and branching tube geometry. Navigation/contact/footer arrows share an authored 24-unit SVG stroke system. The one ambient button has a pill silhouette; links remain text with underlines.
## Components
Ambient control toggles between Pause and Resume, updates aria-pressed, and visibly freezes the pixels. Reduced motion freezes the landscape and disables the control with truthful Still water wording. Navigation is three native anchor links; contact is a direct LinkedIn link in a new tab. Focus uses a 3px rose outline at a 5px offset. A skip link appears on focus.
