I’m Victor, a software developer with experience in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations and ERP. I enjoy turning a tangled requirement into something people can actually use.

The interesting part isn’t only writing code. It’s understanding the need, talking through the tradeoffs, and staying with the work after it leaves my editor.

How I work

STAY WITH
THE PROBLEM.

Requirements, technical design, implementation, testing, deployment, support. I work across the whole path—and across teams—because the handoffs matter as much as the code.

I’m interested in senior and technical-lead roles where careful thinking, practical decisions, and shared understanding make a difference.

  1. UNDERSTAND IT.

    Start with what people need. Ask questions, clarify constraints, and make the requirement specific enough to build.

  2. WORK THROUGH IT.

    Translate the need into technical design and implementation. Test the assumptions as well as the software.

  3. FOLLOW IT THROUGH.

    Make deployment and support part of the work. Coordinate across teams so the solution doesn’t stop at a handoff.
