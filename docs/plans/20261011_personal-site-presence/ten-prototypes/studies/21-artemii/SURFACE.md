# Open Register

Mode: Experience. User-pinned direction, code-led; no concept roll or image-generation requirement.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An indexed visual inventory of practice and interests, not a professional timeline. Bone identity colophon and precise topic index beside a vertical strip of large original artworks on cool black.
OWN-WORLD: Independent typography, page-scale fields and component grammar; no sibling CSS or layouts.
STORY: Discover Victor’s actual scope, read his interests, understand availability and reach genuine LinkedIn.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed pale identity pane and separate inventory left; oversized original plate right. Selecting an index row moves to the associated preview; Open note leads to a complete essay with return preserving selection.
FORM: Explicit pinned reference; seed not applicable. Native anchor/keyboard/touch navigation, normal document scrolling, readable stacked mobile and short landscape.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Six emulated viewports; main, long reading and contact captures. JavaScript-off fallback and instant reduced-motion behavior. Parent owns comparative review and LAN assembly.

## Experience fidelity refinement
The inventory now has All/Practice/Curiosity/Person/Availability filters, authored as a topic-navigation adaptation (not claimed observed source functionality). Real rows and previews are narrowed, with pressed-state feedback; no duplicate scenic cards. Intersection observers synchronize selection with the vertical preview stream. Opening a full note carries its plate through a same-document View Transition; Escape/back restores the exact prior scroll and category filter when returning to that note's origin. Reduced motion is instant and exposes the same content. No ongoing animation loop. Unsupported browsers retain instant state changes.
