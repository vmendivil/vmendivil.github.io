# Night Pavilion — built design system

## Experience fidelity — implemented
`room-camera.js` approaches the selected SVG floor from its actual bay bounds over 680ms, with quartic deceleration. Another room selection interrupts from the current crop; Escape/return restores the 1200×800 whole-house view. `experience.css` removes the prior generic whole-scene scale in favor of this room-specific camera. Room luminance and current index state remain visible. No idle animation loop; hidden tabs finish once then cancel; reduced motion uses the selected crop immediately. The text index is also the phone camera control. Arrival retains a 300px scenic/index band above the reading start so the room approach is actually visible, while focusing the article without further scrolling. This remains an original vector architectural portfolio, not a 3D or real-estate scene.

## Identity
An original SVG timber pavilion provides four direct-access bays: study/About, drafting/Practice, roof atelier/Curiosity, threshold/Contact. Navy-plum night, honey window light and cool silver type translate the live Dwayne Koh portfolio without copying its building, identity, project brands or assets. This is authored vector architectural illustration, not a downloaded 3D model or photoreal render.
## Illustrated material and environmental depth
The preserved slender facade now has graduated side-wall timber joints, roof seams and edge glints, local eave/recess occlusion and room-specific light sources. Atelier light gathers toward its lamp; drafting light pools over its desk; the study lamp warms its chair; the threshold has a dimmer framed doorway. Selective cool glazing reflections retain interior visibility instead of repeating uniformly luminous bays. Low-contrast far hills sit behind thicker foreground shores, overlapping root rocks, terrace paving and dark step risers. The left upright split-fan tree and right swept low crown use independently drawn branches and connected lobed crown masses, with sparse edge foliage—not mirrored particle-stamped trees. This is original vector illustration, not a claim of photorealistic 3D fidelity.

## Behavior
Native anchor hashes expose deep links and browser history. Bay selection brightens the selected SVG group and compresses scenic framing; article focus is programmatic. Escape and Back restore whole composition and initiating-link focus. Evening light changes visible scene luminance. Reduced motion disables transitions but keeps perceptible luminance change.
## Fallback
