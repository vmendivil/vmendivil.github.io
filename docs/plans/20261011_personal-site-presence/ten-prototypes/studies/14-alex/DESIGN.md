# Design System: Practice Ledger

## Overview
**Creative North Star: "Practice Ledger"**
A warm-black professional publication whose material is readable language. Continuous prose, a responsibility spine and marginal line drawings carry the whole visitor path without becoming a card catalogue.
**Key Characteristics:**
- Clear professional sans typography.
- Sparse amber marks, not glow.
- Distinct prose, trail, curiosity and availability grammars.

## Colors
Amber locates responsibility and the practical contact action. Cream carries principal text; secondary warm grey supports ordinary prose. Rules separate stages rather than contain cards.
**The Mark Rule.** Amber marks navigation and responsibility, never decorative dashboard light.

## Typography
Public Sans is bundled with its SIL OFL license. The personal name is large but the reading measure stays calm. Body text is 17px; substantive curiosity text is 16px on mobile. Metadata is subordinate, not the reading surface. Headings use restrained 550 weight.

## Layout
A sticky compact header and ordinary vertical publication. Desktop opening uses two broad columns; About uses heading and prose columns. The practice spine sits left of paired stage and responsibility columns. At 750px these become a single column, with a 22px spine margin. Sections separate by 90px desktop and 58px narrow padding. Mobile navigation remains directly exposed with 44px-high targets; no drawer traps content.

## Elevation & Depth
Flat typographic depth. No shadows, overlays, blurred glass or scene renderer. The responsibility spine and rules establish continuity.

## Shapes
Square publication surfaces. Only 9px responsibility nodes are circular. There is no repeated container-card treatment.

## Components
Native details/summary exposes additional responsibility text. Its plus is CSS linework; Enter and Space toggle, Escape closes the focused disclosure. LinkedIn is a square amber anchor with drawn SVG arrow; hover reverses to cream with dark text. Anchor navigation works without JavaScript. Reduced motion makes scrolling immediate.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do keep continuous prose distinct from the responsibility trail.
- Do preserve an honest availability note and the genuine contact route.
- Don't invent tenure, employers, project outcomes or a resume download.
- Don't move the professional publication into a gallery or a device interface.
