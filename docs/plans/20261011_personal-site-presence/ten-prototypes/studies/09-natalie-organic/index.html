The person behind the studies

Hello, I’m Victor.

I’m a software developer who likes finding a way through complicated things. My experience is grounded in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations and ERP.

I work across requirements, technical design, implementation, testing, deployment, and support—with the coordination that connects them.

Outside that work, I’m drawn to Raspberry Pi homelabs, AI-assisted work processes, and AI-assisted web apps. These studies are a playful way of looking at those interests, not a portfolio of commissioned artwork.

How I approach a problem

First, understand what someone actually needs. Then make the moving parts clearer, choose a workable implementation, test it, and stay attentive to what happens after it ships.

Something to untangle?

I’m full-time employed, and interested in contained work for individuals and small businesses that fits remote, evening, or asynchronous collaboration. I’m also interested in senior and technical-lead opportunities.
