# Design System: Threshold
## Overview
**Creative North Star: "Light Through Complexity"**
Black-green atmospheric depth and original translucent mineral layers carry an intimate personal technology story. Real refractive WebGL artwork is the scene, not professional proof. Reading lives in freestanding statements and pale mineral continuations rather than instrument labels.
**Key Characteristics:**
- Layered asymmetrical optical sculpture; no source arch, ring or flower.
- Ordinary anchors and native disclosures; no required scenic task.
- Authored still-and-prose portrait story for phones and tablets.
## Colors
Cold luminous blue is the optical/focus accent. Moss is a full reading field; dark green text is used over it. Warm green-white supports high-contrast narrative statements. Secondary prose remains visibly readable against deep green.
**The Clear Ground Rule.** Substantial reading belongs on the pale mineral field, not over complex optical edges.
## Typography
Self-hosted Barlow Condensed Semibold gives statements a compact, direct human voice. Desktop display is 96px, chapter headings 80px, reading headings 38px; phones use fluid 52–80px display and 44–68px chapters. Body is 17px, desktop introductory prose 18px, reading line-height 1.75. No tiny HUD copy.
## Layout
Wide scenes at 1050px and above with at least 500px height use a fixed full-viewport WebGL world and min-100svh chapters. Words occupy at most 48%/610px, with opening text grounded low left. Expanded reading becomes a 1060px maximum two-column continuation across the scene. Portrait phones have 340px authored-art compositions, 22px margins and single-column prose; tablets use 480px art and 7vw margins. Short landscape uses art/prose columns and ordinary document scrolling. Native summaries are at least 52px high; links/navigation at least 44px. Fixed direct navigation remains available on portrait and desktop; short landscape uses an ordinary header.
## Elevation & Depth
Actual physical transmission, thin-film iridescence and the preserved seven warped membranes sit between five cohesive eroded mineral masses. World-space interpolated value-noise/fBm supplies non-repeating grain and localized moss; warped sediment seams, a continuous displaced mineral floor, bank-localized talus and PCF-soft directional contact shadows ground the environment. Original fog, optical lights, dust, light shafts and painted radiance environment remain. Reading has one soft offset shadow (0 18px 50px #0004), not a glowing panel.
## Shapes
Sharp unbordered reading continuations contrast with unchanged irregular tapered sheets and cohesive eroded mineral masses. No portal, arch, fern, flower, new page topology or rounded dashboard cards.
## Components
Navigation is a personal name plus three direct anchors, not a menu puzzle. Disclosures are native details/summary with a drawn SVG plus that rotates when expanded; Escape closes and restores summary focus. LinkedIn is an ordinary verified external link, opens in a new tab and says so. Motion button pauses ambient light modulation; scrolling remains available. Reduced motion starts paused and disables smooth scrolling; static light preserves art and reading.
## Do's and Don'ts
- Do keep the artwork original and illustrative.
- Do preserve direct routes, document scroll and comfortable prose.
- Don't use proprietary source art, models or shader code.
- Don't substitute fake coordinates, project evidence or client results for personal truth.
- Don't describe local Chromium checks as physical Safari or whole-ensemble aesthetic approval.
