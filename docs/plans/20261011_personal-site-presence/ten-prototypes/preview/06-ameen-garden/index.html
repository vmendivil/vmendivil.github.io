I’m a software developer and problem solver, grounded in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations and ERP.

I like the moment when a tangled requirement begins to make sense: what people actually need, what the system can do, and how to bring the two together.

My work spans the whole path: requirements, technical design, implementation, testing, deployment, and the support that follows.

Along the way, I coordinate across teams. Software rarely lives on its own.

From tangled
to workable.

Not a magic leap. A thoughtful sequence.

Listen for the real need. Start with the people, the existing process, and the constraints. Make the requirements clear before making the solution complicated.

Find a sensible shape. Connect the technical design to the day-to-day work. I care about a solution people can understand, test, and live with.

Stay with the details. Implementation is only part of the story. Testing, deployment, support, and communication all belong in the work.
