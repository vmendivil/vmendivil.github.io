The study / About Victor

I’m Victor, a software developer who enjoys making complicated systems easier to work with.

My grounding is in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations and ERP. That work puts software close to the way people actually run a business: its requirements, dependencies and everyday problems.

I like following the whole thread, from understanding what is needed to designing, implementing, testing and supporting a solution. The handoffs matter as much as the code.

This pavilion is a small introduction to how I work and what keeps me curious—not a catalogue of client projects.

The drafting bay / Practice

A useful solution starts before implementation and continues after deployment.

Understand the requirement
Make room for questions. Work out what people need, where the current process gets difficult, and what a workable change should do.
Design and implement
Connect the business need to the technical design. Build with the wider ERP system and its dependencies in mind, rather than treating a change as an isolated piece of code.
Test, deploy and support
Check the behavior, coordinate the handoffs and stay attentive to the result in use. Clear communication across teams is part of the technical work.

That end-to-end perspective is what I bring to software and problem-solving. I’m also interested in senior and technical-lead opportunities where that perspective is useful.

The roof atelier / Personal interests

Away from the day-to-day work, I like having a place to try things and understand how they fit together.

A small, self-hosted world

Raspberry Pi, homelab and server interests give me another way to think about software: not just an application, but the machine and environment around it.

AI with a practical purpose

I’m interested in AI-assisted work processes and web applications. The interesting question is where assistance makes the work more useful, understandable or manageable—not where it can add another layer of complexity.

These are personal interests and illustrative explorations, not claims of shipped client projects or measured outcomes.
