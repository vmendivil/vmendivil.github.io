I’m Victor Mendivil. I work on software that has to make sense to the people using it—not just to the person who wrote it.

My experience is grounded in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations and ERP. That means listening to requirements, shaping technical designs, implementing changes and carrying them through testing, deployment and support.

I care about the connections between those stages. A clear requirement helps a test mean something. A considered deployment helps a change become usable. Support brings the next questions back into the design.

I like making complicated things understandable—and finding out how they work in the first place.

Outside my professional work, that curiosity turns toward Raspberry Pi, homelab servers and AI-assisted work processes and web applications. This collection gives those interests room without dressing them up as a list of accomplishments.

Working together

I’m employed full-time. Freelance conversations are best suited to contained needs for individuals or small businesses, with remote, evening or asynchronous collaboration discussed up front. Scope and availability need an honest conversation before any commitment.
