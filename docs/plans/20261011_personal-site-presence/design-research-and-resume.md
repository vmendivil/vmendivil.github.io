## Why the original prototypes failed

Independent researcher diagnosis, accepted by Raptor Dev as a failure of execution rather than a user requirement:

- Every proposal repeated the same three-line slogan silhouette.
- Shared CSS imposed the same wrappers, labels, rules, muted copy, process steps and contact composition.
- Portable system fonts were substituted for an intentional typographic identity.
- No personal imagery or actual visual artifacts established Victor's presence. Diagrams, a rotated quote and an orbital outline substituted generic metaphors.
- Labels and abstract copy remained interchangeable across unrelated personal sites.
- The anti-slop self-audit checked absence of a few clichés, not presence of an individual identity.
- Chromium checks and screenshot inspection were overstated as proof of design quality.

Do not resume by polishing the rejected palette variants or recycling their generator as the foundation for another supposedly independent set.

## Main candidates — not selected yet

### Impeccable — strongest overall candidate

Repository: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable

Design direction plus typography, composition, refinement and independent critique. Its critique separates design judgment from detector/browser findings and asks whether the visual language is specific to the subject.[2][3] New-work instructions distinguish product truth from visual identity and derive materially different structures before selection.[9]

Hermes-specific packaging exists, but full operation uses bundled references and a launcher that can execute/download an engine; runtime compatibility and security have not been verified here.[2] Inspect the full package and execution behavior before authorizing installation. Do not copy SKILL.md alone or run an installer implicitly.

Raptor Dev recommendation: primary workflow candidate. This is NOT VikH's selection or installation approval.

### Hallmark — strongest reference-study candidate

Repository: https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark

Its study flow extracts design principles from URLs/screenshots. It explicitly prioritizes structural variety over skins on the same hero/features/CTA rhythm.[1] The default uses a theme catalog; an explicit custom direction avoids automatically inheriting a catalog theme.[1]

Raptor Dev recommendation: reference-study complement or alternative lead. Respect original creators' assets/identity; screenshot possession alone does not grant reproduction rights. Exact reference choices and custom direction remain undecided.

### Huashu Design — strong visual-exploration candidate

Repository: https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-design

Emphasizes real assets, context-derived form and genuine initial direction drafts with user selection.[6] It explicitly excludes production/SEO sites; scope it to visual exploration, not production implementation.[6]

Cautions from the researcher: Chinese main instructions, extensive supporting bundle, and fallback rules that can permit one layout with typography/palette variants. Do not use that fallback to repeat our pseudo-diversity. Installation/runtime behavior has not been tested.

### Anthropic Frontend Design — lightweight alternative

Repository and skill: https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design

Current instructions derive choices from the subject and audience and explicitly warn against accented headline words, unnecessary uppercase labels, monospace chrome and interchangeable editorial layouts.[4]

Less tooling than Impeccable, but quality depends on executing its plan/review/build/critique method well. Not independently tested on this site.

### Taste-Skill — conditional challenger

Repository: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill

Targets landing pages/portfolios, reads audience/reference signals and requires imagery.[5] Its style/motion/density dials, stack assumptions and categorical image prescriptions may impose another recognizable house style.[5]

Do not allow defaults to dictate a framework migration, unnecessary animation, fabricated proof or irrelevant generated imagery. Not the first recommendation for this brief, but remains a candidate.

## Proposed quality guidelines — discuss/approve the approach before execution

The imagery/credibility requirement above is confirmed. The following is Raptor Dev's proposed method, NOT an approved implementation plan:

- Study real references and identify the compositional principles that fit Victor; don't select a fashionable preset first.
- Obtain purposeful visual material: potentially a portrait, approved authentic work/home-lab images or deliberately commissioned assets. Actual assets, rights and sourcing remain unresolved. No synthetic people presented as real, fabricated client work or random stock used as identity.

- Make image selection, crop, quality, responsive treatment and licensing first-class design decisions.
- Show visual directions early for human feedback before completing full pages. Number and fidelity of new directions remain to be agreed; the earlier request was for five.
- Separate unanchored independent visual critique from technical browser checks; both matter, neither replaces VikH's taste judgment.
- Avoid loading several competing workflow controllers simultaneously. Select a primary skill and only specific complements if useful.
- Preserve factual copy constraints and dark-theme preference. Don't invent social proof to make a composition work.
- Never claim an unrecognizably-AI guarantee or measured improvement without evidence.
